# Selected Easy Stay identity

Status: selected Happy Home direction, with colour and black-and-white versions requested by the user. Repository assembled 22 October 2026.

## Logo

Use the roof-and-smile Happy Home symbol and the rounded lowercase easy stay wordmark. Use the primary lockup for general branding, horizontal lockup for narrow headers, and standalone icon for small spaces. The ES monogram and Open Door concepts are archived alternatives.

The wordmark uses outlined Century Gothic Bold lettering. It is not Airbnb's proprietary typeface. SVG lettering is outlined, so display does not require a font installation. Manrope remains a proposed interface typeface in the strategy, separate from the logo.

Check legibility at the final display size; minimum-size and clear-space standards still need production validation.
